Zennith Vivy Media

A personal backup tool that keeps a copy of its owner's media archive in the owner's own Google Drive.

What it does

Zennith Vivy Media runs on a private server operated by its owner. It collects media files from accounts the owner manages, checks that each file is complete, and uploads it to a dedicated folder in the owner's Google Drive. After the upload is confirmed with a checksum, the local copy is removed.

Who can use it

Zennith Vivy Media is not a public service. It is used only by its owner, and it only accesses the owner's own Google account.

Google Drive access

The app requests a single Google permission: access to files that the app itself creates in Google Drive (the drive.file scope). It cannot see or change any other files in the account.
